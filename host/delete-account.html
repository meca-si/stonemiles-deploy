Delete your STONEMILES account

You can ask us to delete your STONEMILES by Stonervision account at any time. Deleting the account removes your sign-in, your profile, and the data tied to your account (trips, loads, records, and documents you saved in the app).

How to request account deletion

Email stonervisionteam@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We verify the request comes from the account owner, then delete the account and its data and confirm by email when it is done.

If you only want some of your data removed and want to keep the account, see Delete your data instead.

Some records may be kept where the law requires it (for example, payment and tax records); everything else tied to the account is removed.
