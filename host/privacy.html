STONEMILES Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
App: STONEMILES by Stonervision
Package: com.stonervision.stonemiles
Privacy contact: stonervisionteam@gmail.com

STONEMILES by Stonervision (“STONEMILES,” “we,” “us” or “our”) provides truck navigation, trip and load recordkeeping, mileage reporting and related tools for commercial drivers and fleets. This policy explains what information the app collects, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to you.

Operated by: RUGGS LLC

Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Name and email address;
  • Account identifier, driver/fleet role and profile settings;
  • Subscription plan, entitlement status and related account preferences;
  • Messages you send to support.

Location information

Location is core to STONEMILES. With your permission, the app collects precise device location, speed, heading and related route information to provide navigation, truck-aware routing, speed and road alerts, trip recording, state-line mileage and other location-based features.

Location is collected only while the app is in use (in the foreground), while you are actively navigating or recording a trip. The app does not collect your location in the background.

If you deny or limit location permission, navigation, automatic trip functions and other location-based features may not work correctly. You can change location permissions in your device settings.

Trip, load and business records

We collect information you enter, import, scan or generate while using the app, which may include:

  • Trip origins, destinations, routes, odometer readings and state-crossing mileage;
  • Trip dates and times, driving status and planning information;
  • Load numbers, pickup and delivery details, business names and addresses, notes and record status;
  • Mileage, pay, expense or settlement information you choose to enter for your own records;
  • Documents or images you choose to attach to a trip or load record, such as a signed bill of lading or proof-of-delivery photo;
  • Exports and reports you create, including IFTA mileage reports.

If you receive a delivery confirmation link from a driver, the name you type, any notes you add and any photo you submit are recorded with that delivery record. The confirmation page never shows the load’s rate or payment details.

Device, diagnostic and usage information

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate and secure the app, such as:

  • Device model, operating-system version and app version;
  • Language, time-zone and app settings;
  • Crash logs, diagnostic events and performance information — the app can report its own crashes and failures to us so we can fix them;
  • Internet Protocol address and account/session security identifiers;
  • Interactions with app features and settings.

Subscription and payment information

Paid plans are purchased through Google Play unless another authorised purchase channel is expressly offered in the app. Google processes the payment. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number from Google Play purchases.

We may receive and store subscription information such as the plan purchased, purchase/subscription status, renewal or expiry status, transaction identifiers and country/store information needed to provide and support the entitlement.

Wallet and staged financial functions

The app includes wallet-related screens by design, but external crypto swap and fiat transaction rails are turned off in this release unless and until we expressly enable them in the app. We never receive or store your seed phrase, private key or recovery code, and we will never ask for them by email, support message or any other channel.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide navigation, routing, speed-limit and road-awareness features;
  • Record, organise, seal, synchronise and export trips, loads and mileage;
  • Provide HOS planning information and trip history;
  • Operate subscriptions and account entitlements;
  • Provide fleet features where an authorised fleet relationship exists;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot problems;
  • Improve app reliability and features;
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting and safety obligations; and
  • Communicate important service, account and policy information.

We do not use precise location, trip records or load records for third-party advertising, and we never sell your personal information, precise location, trip records or load records.

Sealed and hashed records

Some trip and load records can be “sealed” using a cryptographic hash or hash chain. A hash is a digital fingerprint used to help detect whether the sealed content was changed after the seal was created.

A hash is not the same thing as encryption and does not, by itself, make the underlying record private. Record content is stored in your account on our servers and protected by account controls and other security measures.

When information is shared

We share information only as described below:

  • Service providers: Vendors that process information on our behalf — for example our hosting and database provider, our email delivery provider (account invites and password resets), and mapping, routing and weather data providers that return results for your route.
  • People and organisations you direct us to serve: For example, if you export, email or otherwise send a trip sheet, load record or report to a recipient you choose.
  • Fleet relationships: If your account is part of a fleet, the fleet owner and authorised dispatch users can see the loads assigned to you, their status, delivery confirmations and the fleet’s related records in the fleet library.
  • Legal and safety reasons: If disclosure is required by law, legal process or a valid governmental request, or is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security or the integrity of the service.
  • Business transfer: If the business or app is sold, merged or transferred, information may be transferred subject to this policy or a successor policy that provides comparable protection.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share precise location or trip/load records with data brokers or advertising networks.

Your choices and controls

Depending on your account and device, you may be able to:

  • Review and update profile and account information in the app;
  • Change location, notification, camera, file and other device permissions in Android settings;
  • Stop an active trip or route recording using the controls in the app;
  • Export certain trip, load and mileage records;
  • Request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information by contacting stonervisionteam@gmail.com.

Delete your account: https://app.stonervision.io/delete-account
Delete your data: https://app.stonervision.io/delete-data

If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or de-identify information that we are not required or permitted to retain for legal, tax, accounting, security, dispute or backup purposes. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being overwritten or deleted.

Retention

We retain account information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service. Trip, load and mileage records are retained until you delete them, delete your account, or ask us to delete them, subject to the legal and operational exceptions described above.

We retain subscription and transaction records as needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and support. Diagnostic and security records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for reliability, security and troubleshooting.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for data transmitted between the app and our services. No Internet, device or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children and intended users

STONEMILES is designed for adult commercial drivers and fleet operators. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact stonervisionteam@gmail.com so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

International use

The app and its services are operated from the United States, and information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. Privacy laws in those places may differ from the laws where you are located.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, our practices or applicable law changes. If we make a material change, we will update the effective date and provide notice through the app, the policy page or another reasonable method before or when the change takes effect where required.
