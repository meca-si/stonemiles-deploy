Delete your STONEMILES data

You do not have to delete your account to remove your data. In the app you can delete individual trips, loads, and other records you created wherever the app shows a delete option for them.

Delete all of your data

To have all data tied to your account removed while keeping the account itself, email stonervisionteam@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my data”. We verify the request comes from the account owner, remove the data, and confirm by email when it is done.

To close the account as well, see Delete your account.

Some records may be kept where the law requires it (for example, payment and tax records); everything else covered by the request is removed.
